01 / Security response

Following the evidence.
Recovering $10,000.

Skyward Technical Solutions · Client incident

  • Email security
  • Log analysis
  • Incident investigation

I investigated an email compromise after a client’s employee scanned a malicious QR code that had passed through their email filter.

Reviewing logs, I found that the attacker had sent several requests to the client’s billing team and created a mailbox rule to hide replies to those messages.

The result

I uncovered the attacker’s activity and recovered $10,000 for the client.

04 / Custom development

Credit data into
a clearer overview.

NetRecoveries · Lead Programmer

  • JavaScript
  • Data parsing
  • Business rules

I created custom JavaScript scripts to help auto loan companies categorize applicants and review incoming applications.

The scripts parsed data from credit reporting agencies, including Equifax, and organized applicants using variables such as credit scores and outstanding debts. I worked closely with managers to fine-tune the rules around the factors they considered most important.

The result

A quick overview of incoming applications that helped loan officers assess risk, with categorization tailored to each company’s priorities.
